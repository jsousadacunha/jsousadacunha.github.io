This privacy policy for J.M. Sousa ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") describes how we handle your data. Our site does not collect any personal information, neither directly nor indirectly. It does not display ads, nor does it have tiers or user accounts.

## Data We Collect

We only collect aggregate data for statistical purposes:

The total number of visits to our site.
The country of origin of visitors, based solely on IP address geolocation.
This information is used exclusively to monitor site usage and improve our service. No personal data, such as names, contact details, or device identifiers, are collected, stored, or processed.

## Legal Compliance

Our practices respect the European GDPR and similar privacy regulations. Since we do not process personal data, user consent or user agreements are not required.

## Security and Data Sharing

Because no personal information is collected, there is no risk of data leaks or breaches of user privacy related to personal data. We do not share any information with third parties, as only aggregated, anonymous statistics are used.

## Data Retention

The collected aggregated data is stored only for as long as necessary to monitor site performance, typically a maximum of 90 days, after which it is securely deleted.

## Changes to This Privacy Notice

We may update this notice to reflect any changes in our data practices or applicable law. Notices of substantial changes will be posted here.

## Contact

As we do not process or store personal information, there is no contact point for data privacy inquiries.
